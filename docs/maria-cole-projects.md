(seeded by `scripts/seed-demo.ts`). No Classroom: visitors build their own
4th workshop on their visit. Every project is **working** (fitting spec +
records) and sits
at the final step of its kind, so visitors can click back through the
steps (back buttons on every screen) and see how each one comes out.
Final steps: hardware It arrived · software Downloaded · print Collect ·
music Release · dance Perform · recipe Cook.

Takes effect on the next `pnpm run seed:demo`.
